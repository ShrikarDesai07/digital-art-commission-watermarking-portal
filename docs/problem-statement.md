# Problem Statement #58 — Digital Art Commission & Watermarking Portal

**Domain:** Media, Events & Community

An art commission platform where clients submit visual creative briefs, artists submit watermarked WIP progress drafts, and final high-resolution source files unlock upon milestone payment.

**Actors:** Client Buyer, Digital Artist

### Source requirements supplied by the lab handout
- Draft illustrations uploaded by artists must receive a dynamic diagonal overlay watermark before being displayed to the client.
- Final high-resolution asset downloads must use expiring signed S3 URLs valid for 60 minutes only.
- The portal must support the commission, WIP review, milestone-payment, and final-asset release workflow.
